(size 60, release 2026.02.25, https://wordlist.aspell.net), produced by
applying its affix rules to every stem. It is redistributed under SCOWL's

SCOWL is derived from many sources, most of which are in the Public Domain.
The primary source of words for SCOWL comes from 12dicts and ENABLE2K.  Both
are in the Public Domain, but Alan Beale <biljir@pobox.com> deserves special
credit as he is the author of 12dicts and a major contributor to ENABLE2K.

The affix rules used to expand the stems are from the en_US.aff that ships
with the hunspell dictionary, a modified version of Geoff Kuenning's Ispell
english.aff (Copyright 1993, Geoff Kuenning, Granada Hills, CA; BSD licence).
Only the expanded word forms are included here, not the affix file itself.
